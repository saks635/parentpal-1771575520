About the Startup

AI-powered parenting assistant named ParentPal. Provides personalized tips on child development, schedules, and educational activities. Tracks milestones, sends reminders for vaccinations, and suggests healthy routines. Includes AI-driven content recommendations for kids aged 0-12. Target audience: parents aged 25-40 seeking support for early childhood development and parenting guidance.
